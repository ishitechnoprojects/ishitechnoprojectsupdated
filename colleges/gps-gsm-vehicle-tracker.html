📍 GPS/GSM Vehicle Tracker

Fleet owners lack real-time visibility into vehicle location and status.

GPS/GSM Final Year

Problem Statement

Fleet owners lack real-time visibility into vehicle location and status.

Objective

To design and implement a working gps/gsm vehicle tracker that demonstrates practical GPS/GSM concepts with real hardware and measurable results.

Hardware Required

ESP32, GPS module, GSM/Wi-Fi connectivity

Software Required

Firebase Realtime Database, live web dashboard

Features

  • Live location tracking
  • Geofencing and overspeed alerts
  • Same technology behind our IshiTrackers platform

We provide full support — hardware assembly, firmware/code, block diagram, working-principle explanation and viva preparation.
