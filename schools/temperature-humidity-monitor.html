🌡️ Temperature & Humidity Monitor

Shows the current room temperature and humidity on a small display.

⭐ Beginner Suitable for: School Students / Science Exhibition

Overview

A simple weather-station-style project that continuously reads and displays the temperature and humidity of any room.

How It Works

The DHT11 sensor takes temperature and humidity readings and sends them to the Arduino, which then prints the numbers on an LCD screen, refreshing every couple of seconds.

Components Required

DHT11 sensor, 16x2 LCD display, Arduino Uno

Working Principle

DHT11 uses a thermistor for temperature and a humidity-sensing capacitor, both converted into digital signals the Arduino can read directly.

What You'll Learn

Digital sensors and how to show live readings on a screen.

Project Features

  • Real-time readings
  • Useful for greenhouses, labs and homes
  • Clear LCD display, easy to read

Optional Upgrades

  • Log data over time on an SD card
  • Send readings to a mobile app over Wi-Fi
  • Add a fan that turns on automatically when it's too hot
