🌱 Automatic Plant Watering

Waters your plant by itself whenever the soil gets too dry.

⭐ Beginner Suitable for: School Students / Science Exhibition

Overview

Never forget to water your plants again — this system checks the soil moisture and switches on a small pump to water the plant automatically when it's too dry.

How It Works

A soil moisture sensor is inserted into the plant's soil. When the reading shows the soil is dry, the Arduino activates a relay which turns on a small water pump for a short time.

Components Required

Soil moisture sensor, Mini water pump, Relay module, Arduino Uno, Water tubing

Working Principle

Dry soil has higher electrical resistance than wet soil, so the sensor's reading changes with moisture content.

What You'll Learn

Environmental sensing and simple automation that solves a real everyday problem.

Project Features

  • Keeps plants healthy without daily attention
  • Prevents both over- and under-watering
  • Easy to scale to multiple pots

Optional Upgrades

  • Add a rain sensor to skip watering on rainy days
  • Add solar power for outdoor gardens
  • Send a phone notification each time it waters
