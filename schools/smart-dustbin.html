🗑️ Smart Dustbin

A dustbin whose lid opens automatically when your hand comes near it.

⭐ Beginner Suitable for: School Students / Science Exhibition

Overview

No more touching a dirty dustbin lid! This smart bin detects your hand and opens its lid automatically, then closes it again after a few seconds.

How It Works

An ultrasonic sensor measures the distance to any object in front of it. When something (like your hand) comes within a set distance, the Arduino tells a servo motor to lift the lid.

Components Required

Ultrasonic sensor, Servo motor, Arduino Uno, Dustbin body

Working Principle

Ultrasonic sensors send out a sound pulse and measure how long it takes to bounce back — this time is converted into a distance.

What You'll Learn

Distance sensing and how to move a motor to a precise angle using code.

Project Features

  • Touch-free and hygienic
  • Automatically closes after use
  • Compact and easy to build into any small bin

Optional Upgrades

  • Add a fill-level sensor to alert when the bin is full
  • Add a solar-powered battery pack
  • Add an app notification for cleaning staff
