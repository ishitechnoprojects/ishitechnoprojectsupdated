🚗 Mini Robotic Car

A simple remote or app-controlled car built from scratch with Arduino.

⭐ Beginner Suitable for: School Students / Science Exhibition

Overview

A fun first robotics build — drive this little car forward, backward, left and right using a Bluetooth app on your phone.

How It Works

Commands sent from a Bluetooth app on your phone are received by the Bluetooth module and passed to the Arduino, which then drives the motors in the corresponding direction.

Components Required

Motor driver, 2-4 DC motors, Bluetooth module, Chassis kit, Arduino Uno

Working Principle

Bluetooth serial communication sends simple text/byte commands (like 'F' for forward) which the Arduino code interprets into motor actions.

What You'll Learn

Basic motor control and wireless communication between a phone and a microcontroller.

Project Features

  • Fun, hands-on introduction to robotics
  • Full manual control from a smartphone
  • Easily upgraded into an autonomous robot later

Optional Upgrades

  • Add obstacle-avoidance sensors
  • Add a camera for first-person driving
  • Upgrade to Wi-Fi control with ESP32
