🔥 Fire Alarm

Sounds a loud alarm the moment it detects flame or excess heat.

⭐ Beginner Suitable for: School Students / Science Exhibition

Overview

A safety demonstration project that detects a nearby flame and immediately triggers a loud buzzer and flashing LED to warn everyone in the room.

How It Works

A flame sensor detects infrared light emitted by fire, which is different from ordinary light. When flame is detected, the Arduino instantly activates the buzzer.

Components Required

Flame sensor, Buzzer, LED, Arduino Uno

Working Principle

Flame sensors are tuned to detect the specific infrared wavelength range produced by burning materials.

What You'll Learn

Safety-focused sensing and how quick alerts can prevent accidents.

Project Features

  • Fast response time
  • Simple, reliable safety demo
  • Great topic for a safety-awareness exhibition

Optional Upgrades

  • Add an automatic water sprinkler trigger
  • Send an SMS alert to a phone
  • Add a smoke sensor for earlier detection
