Who we are

From classroom projects to production hardware

Ishitechno Projects is a technology-driven company focused on designing and developing Embedded Systems, IoT Solutions, Artificial Intelligence applications, PCB Design, and Custom Electronic Products. Our mission is to transform innovative ideas into reliable and scalable technology solutions.

We serve students, startups, industries, research organizations, and government departments by delivering high-quality engineering solutions, prototypes, and production-ready products. From hardware design to firmware development and cloud integration, we provide complete end-to-end support — including our own GPS tracking platform, IshiTrackers.
